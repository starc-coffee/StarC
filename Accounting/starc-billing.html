Where is my data stored?

Every invoice is saved (the data folder next to this page), so you see the same records on any device. If the internet drops, invoices are kept on the device and sent automatically once it is back (the badge at the top shows what is waiting). The server also keeps an automatic daily copy of the records file. The Excel backup is an extra copy you can keep yourself — download it regularly.
